User Guide: How It Works

1
Setup Corporate Branding: Go to Settings ➔ Vantis CRM + Marketing and enter your company name, marketing email, support phone, and logo text. The AI copywriter reads these parameters in real-time to tailor all email footers and WhatsApp templates dynamically.

2
Configure Salesperson Gmail: Each salesperson goes to their "My Profile / Preferences" screen, enters their Gmail IMAP connection credentials, and clicks the "Probar conexión" (Test Connection) button to receive instant success feedback from Odoo.

3
Design Campaigns with AI copywriting: Create a Campaign inside the new top-level menu "Campañas de Márketing", choose your channels (Email, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn), select your target stage, and pull leads. Create an AI template, design your hook prompt, and click **"✨ Generar contenido IA"** to compose personalized copies for every single lead in background batches.
4
Synchronize and Launch Prosp direct: If you checked LinkedIn, click **"Sincronizar Opciones de Prosp"**. Choose your campaign and target list from the elegant dropdown menus (retrieved live using Prosp's MCP JSON-RPC protocol!). Click **"Aprobar todo"** and then **"Lanzar en Prosp"**. Odoo will asynchronously stagger and drip-upload 15 leads every 10 minutes to protect your LinkedIn account from WAF/Cloudflare rate-limits.
